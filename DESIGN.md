## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the ethos of understated architectural luxury and artisanal craftsmanship. Catering to discerning private clients, architects, and luxury property developers, the interface mirrors the experience of walking through a bespoke, curated residential sanctuary. 

The aesthetic synthesizes warm minimalism with quiet luxury and tactile materiality:
- **Refined Architectural Proportions:** Clean planar compositions, unhurried negative space, and disciplined spatial balance.
- **Sensory Materiality:** Visual cues evoking textured lime plaster, brushed champagne brass, oiled walnut, and fluted acoustic glass.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Radiant, soft-focus surfaces replacing cold high-tech surfaces with warm, inviting intimacy.

## Colors
The palette draws direct inspiration from natural interior architectural materials:
- **Primary (`#2B1E16` Deep Walnut / Espresso):** Anchors typographic hierarchy, structural silhouettes, and sharp contrast elements.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` Champagne Muted Gold):** Reserved for focal gestures, subtle status highlights, active pagination states, and delicate metadata accents.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7256` Weathered Umber):** Supports secondary typography, quiet icons, and refined micro-copy.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` Alabaster / Cream):** Forms the foundational luminous canvas, avoiding harsh digital white in favor of soft, natural lime-wash depth. Surface tiers stack softly between warm alabaster, almond stone (`#F3EEE6`), and subtle linen layers.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances editorial grandeur with functional legibility:
- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`. Titles lean on deliberate editorial kerning, loose leading, and classical Roman proportions. Large display headings should embrace italic styles for contextual emphasis or project curation tags.
- **Body & Interface:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Generous line heights ensure frictionless readability across extended editorial project narratives, material specifications, and design philosophies.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Styled with uppercase treatment and deliberate letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror archival architectural prints and gallery placards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects a calm, uncrowded spatial framework inspired by contemporary architectural floor plans:
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by expansive outer margins. 
  - **Desktop (>=1200px):** 12 columns, `2rem` gutters, and `4rem` outer canvas margins. Full-bleed imagery occasionally breaks the grid to provide tactile immersion.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2.5rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins with disciplined vertical pacing.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals are favored over dense clusters. Sections breathe with staggered asymmetry, honoring negative space as a primary luxury material.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is subtle, diffuse, and luminous, prioritizing architectural surface layering over deep drop shadows:
- **Frosted Glass:** Floating headers, filter bars, and modal overlays utilize translucent alabaster planes (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.78)`) combined with a heavy backdrop blur (`20px - 28px`) and ultra-fine border definition (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)`).
- **Subtle Ambient Shadows:** Elevated cards and interactive overlays cast gossamer-soft, golden-tinted ambient glows (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.06)`).
- **Hairline Dividers:** Section delineations rely on whisper-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.08)` or `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)`) echoing delicate metal joinery.

## Shapes
Shapes favor pure, rectilinear poise paired with softened architectural corners (`0.25rem` baseline). 
- Surfaces convey stone-cut precision rather than casual rounded bubbles.
- Buttons, input bounds, and photography frames maintain architectural discipline.
- Select interactive status pills and metadata badges may adapt full pill geometries exclusively when mirroring tactile physical tags.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid deep walnut background (`#2B1E16`), warm cream text (`#FAF7F2`), subtle transition to softened umber hover (`#3D2D22`).
  - *Secondary:* Translucent parchment background, framed with hairline brushed champagne border (`1px solid #C5A880`), dark walnut label.
  - *Text/Editorial:* Understated serif or tracked sans label with an animated baseline stroke that extends on hover.

- **Cards (Portfolios & Collections):**
  - Crisp aspect ratios (4:5 or 16:11), full-bleed imagery framed with micro-borders (`1px solid rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.06)`).
  - Floating metadata cards in frosted almond glass hovering gracefully over bottom imagery edges.
  - Hover states trigger subtle image scale (1.02x) accompanied by gentle focus shifts.

- **Input Fields & Forms:**
  - Minimal underline or enclosed low-contrast boxes (`#F3EEE6`) with transparent inner fills.
  - Floating labels in `label-sm` with champagne focus rings (`1px solid #C5A880`), strictly avoiding standard harsh blue focus states.

- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Featherweight containers tinted in soft almond stone, enclosed by fine hairline borders with `label-sm` typography to indicate architectural styles, spaces, or materials.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Architectural square or disc geometries. Unselected states feature delicate walnut borders (`rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.3)`); selected states fill with deep walnut and champagne accents.

- **Curated Additions (Material Swatches & Lookbook Carousels):**
  - Circular tactile finish swatches (e.g., Travertine, Bouclé, Walnut, Satin Brass) highlighted by an outer floating gold boundary when active.
  - Full-screen imagery viewports equipped with bespoke thin-line drag indicators and numbered pagination counters (`01 / 08`).
